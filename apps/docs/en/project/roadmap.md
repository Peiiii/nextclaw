# NextClaw Roadmap

NextClaw aims to become a proactive, continuously improving, long-term AI partner, with one fundamental mission: to be a good partner to its user. See the [Vision](/en/project/vision).

**The goal for 1.0: deliver a long-term AI partner that understands you, takes useful initiative, and continuously improves itself.**

The four stages below describe planned work toward 1.0. Full acceptance testing is not complete, and no release date is set. Development will build on existing memory, scheduling, conversations, skills, and result delivery; these are neither all-new systems nor capabilities that are all available today. See [Release Notes](/en/project/release-notes) for shipped features.

## Stage 1: Understand You and Your Goals

Help your partner keep track of your background, goals, and current circumstances, so a new conversation can still use the latest relevant information.

| Planned feature | What it will let you do |
| --- | --- |
| Partner identity and ongoing participation settings | Decide what it may proactively participate in, which material it may use, and which actions it may take; turn initiative off at any time |
| Automatic organization of user information | Build an understanding of your background, preferences, and circumstances through normal conversation, distinguishing confirmed facts from interpretations that need confirmation |
| Review, correction, and expiry of remembered information | Inspect its understanding, correct mistakes or stop using a fact, and reconfirm temporary circumstances when they expire |
| Personal goals and plans across time horizons | Manage long-term goals, intermediate plans, near-term actions, priorities, and progress, including goals that do not belong to a project |
| Relevant context across conversations and background work | Use current, relevant, authorized background in new conversations and background analysis, with an explanation of what informed a suggestion |

## Stage 2: Proactive Analysis, Planning, and Conversation

Without a new request, your partner should be able to revisit existing information at an appropriate time, share a relevant change, or reach out with something worth discussing.

| Planned feature | What it will let you do |
| --- | --- |
| Automatic identification and management of ongoing interests | Keep track of goals, circumstances, and unresolved questions; see why something matters, recent results, and follow-up arrangements, with pause and stop controls |
| Background analysis and planning | Notice conflicts between goals and plans, compare options, and offer suggestions; gain useful new insights even when nothing external has changed |
| Timing and frequency controls | Set quiet hours, frequency, and budget; stay quiet when there is no new value, avoid repeated reminders, and pause initiative |
| Proactive topics and continued conversation | Reply directly, discuss later, dismiss once, or stop future outreach on a topic; keep discussing a question without having to make a decision immediately |
| Action on suggestions and result follow-up | Carry out work when you accept or existing authorization allows it, deliver artifacts, and update related plans, progress, and ongoing interests |

For example, it might notice that your weekly commitments exceed your available time, compare two ways to adjust them, and ask about priorities. Thinking something through, planning ahead, or learning something important can be a complete contribution without turning the conversation into an execution task.

## Stage 3: Continuous Self-Improvement

Let experience and feedback improve the next interaction and reduce repeated mistakes. Self-improvement always serves the purpose of being a better partner to you.

| Planned feature | What it will let you do |
| --- | --- |
| Automatic reflection on outcomes and feedback | Identify useful lessons from failures, unsatisfactory results, and corrections without an extra request to reflect each time |
| Classification and application of experience | Update user understanding, participation preferences, or working methods in the appropriate place, and apply them in later relevant situations |
| Improvement history and results | See what changed, why, when it was applied, and what happened; clearly distinguish changes whose effects are still unverified |
| Correction, disabling, and reversal of experience | Correct a mistaken lesson, disable an unsuitable strategy, or undo a change while preserving edits you made afterward |

## Stage 4: Easy to Start and Reliable Over Time

| Planned feature | What it will let you do |
| --- | --- |
| Partner setup and capability status | Provide necessary background, confirm a goal and participation scope, and see which capabilities your entry point supports, whether background work is running, and why it is paused |
| Recovery and upgrades | Keep user understanding, plans, ongoing interests, and improvement history across restarts, connection failures, and upgrades; see failures and avoid duplicate notifications or actions on recovery |

Persistence, stop controls, and permissions accompany each stage. Stage 4 checks their reliability during continued use. Support across runtimes and channels will follow actual validation; full 1.0 capabilities are not promised for every entry point at once.

## How We Will Assess 1.0

Completing the features is followed by these core acceptance cases and continuous user trials. These are requirements, not results that have already passed.

| Core case | Required result |
| --- | --- |
| Understanding survives new conversations and changed plans | Relevant background is used, corrections take effect, and expired or excluded facts no longer drive suggestions |
| Useful initiative without a new instruction | Independently analyze existing information and start a useful discussion, report relevant changes, and act when appropriate; stay quiet without new value |
| A real problem improves later behavior | A genuine correction or error leads to a traceable improvement that helps in another relevant situation and can be reversed |
| Reliable continuation after interruptions | Stop requests, revoked permissions, restarts, and upgrades preserve correct state without false success or duplicate actions |

## After 1.0

Actual usage will guide broader data sources, services, runtimes, and device support, along with better judgment about initiative and improvement. Plugins, skills, SDKs, and the ecosystem will continue extending what your partner can do. Ordering and dates will be updated separately.
