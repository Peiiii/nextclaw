# 2026-08-18 · NextClaw v0.39.2

Published: 2026-08-18
Tags: `release` `npm` `patch`

NextClaw v0.39.2 fixes Marketplace Panel Apps failing to reopen after being pinned to the right sidebar.

## Fixes

- Pinned entries now use the stable Panel App ID instead of a path tied to one installation.
- Reopening a Marketplace Panel App from the right sidebar now resolves the currently enabled installation instead of showing `PANEL_APP_NOT_FOUND`.

## Defaults and compatibility

- Existing pinned entries automatically follow the active version after an upgrade, rollback, or reinstall.
- Disabled or uninstalled Apps do not run files left behind on disk; the original pinned entry works again after the App is enabled or installed again.
- Workspace Panel Apps and explicitly opened local Panel Apps keep their existing behavior.

Learn more: [NextClaw Apps](/en/guide/panel-apps)
