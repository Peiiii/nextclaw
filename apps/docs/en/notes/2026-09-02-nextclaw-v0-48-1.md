# NextClaw v0.48.1

Released: 2026-09-02  
Tags: Windows, Desktop, session recovery

NextClaw v0.48.1 fixes session recovery after a Windows Desktop upgrade. If you upgraded from v0.47.0 or earlier and your existing sessions were still missing on v0.48.0, this update restores the session list and messages from the durable local history during startup.

## What is fixed

- Restores session-list entries and existing messages lost from view after an upgrade.
- Preserves sessions you intentionally deleted instead of showing them again.
- Continues writing new messages to the same local history so they remain available after a restart.

## What you need to do

Update to v0.48.1 and restart the desktop app. You do not need to delete local data, reinstall the app, or import sessions manually.
