# 2026-08-20 · NextClaw v0.40.1

Published: 2026-08-20
Tags: `release` `npm` `patch`

NextClaw v0.40.1 completes the management experience for Panel Apps pinned to the main sidebar.

## Fixes

- The Panel Apps list, running view, and left main sidebar now expose the same “Add to main sidebar” and “Remove from main sidebar” action.
- In the expanded left sidebar, hover a Panel App entry to open its menu and remove it without closing the current App page.
- Panel Apps reopened from older sessions or restored state now resolve their identity correctly instead of losing the main-sidebar action.
- Panel Apps use the same page component and navigation history regardless of where they were opened.

Main-sidebar bindings remain persisted in NextClaw preferences. No migration or re-pinning is required after upgrading.
